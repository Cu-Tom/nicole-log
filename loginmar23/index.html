Lesson Summary (March 23, lesson22)

In this lesson, we learned about the concept of tolerances and how to control the fit between parts in the design and manufacturing process. Then, we learned the modeling techniques of Rhino software, including the method of creating surfaces from plane curves, and the union and difference operations in Boolean operations. In the practical session, we completed a box with a lid through Rhino modeling and learned how to do skeleton design.
